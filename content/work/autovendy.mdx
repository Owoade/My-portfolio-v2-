## Problem

Buying and selling cars is still a scattered, trust-heavy process: listings sit across dealer lots, social media pages, and classified sites, each with its own contact method and no reliable history on the vehicle or the seller. Buyers waste time chasing stale or misrepresented listings, sellers field endless calls from unserious buyers, and there is no single trusted place where a car deal can be found, evaluated, and closed.

## Solution

Autovendy, Carmax for Africa, a platform that brings car buying and selling into one online marketplace: sellers list their vehicles with structured details and photos, buyers browse, compare, and reach sellers from a single trusted venue, and the listing-to-sale process lives in one place instead of scattered channels.
