## Problem

Traders make decisions on insight, but that insight is scattered across separate tools: charts in one tab, heatmaps in another, order books in a third, plus news and on-chain or market data elsewhere. Stitching these together by hand is slow, and in fast markets the delay costs entries and exits. Constant tab-switching also splits attention, so signals get missed, context is lost, and decisions rest on partial pictures.

## Solution

Claw is an all-in-one trading terminal that unites charting, heatmaps, order book depth, and market data in a single workspace, and lets traders place orders right where they spot the opportunity. Insight and execution happen on one screen, so no tab-switching and no delay between decision and trade.


## Results

More than 3,000 traders have used Claw to analyze markets and place trades.
