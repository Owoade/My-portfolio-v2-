7. CASE STUDY TEMPLATE  (/work/[slug])
======================================================================
MDX frontmatter:
  title, slug, summary, role, timeline, year, stack[], links{live,repo},
  cover, metrics[{label,value}], featured(bool)

Page sections in order:
  1. Header: title, role, timeline, stack tags, live link
  2. Key metrics bar (amber numbers)
  3. Context        (1 short paragraph: what it is, who it is for)
  4. Problem        (what was broken or missing, who felt it)
  5. Constraints    (scale, budget, deadlines, infra realities)
  6. Solution       (approach plus architecture diagram)
  7. Key decisions  (3 to 5 decisions with tradeoffs)
  8. Result         (numbers first, then qualitative outcome)
  9. Learnings      (what I would do differently)
 10. Next / previous case study navigation

Diagrams: use Mermaid or static SVG, dark-theme friendly.
